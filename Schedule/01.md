## BattleBuild 
*Activity - Scenario 1 - Silo Model and Send*

In class experiment to simulate the challenges of multidisciplinary BIM based collaboration in projects. Are your assumptions about good BIM enough?
* ___ You will be assigned a role in a project team, this could be a BIM manager or an engineer / architect.
* ___ You will be assigned one of 2 different software options for your role.
* ___ You will have a task to complete related to your role before the deadline.
* ___ Can you complete both your own goals and that of the project?
* ___ Submit your final design for review when you are complete.
